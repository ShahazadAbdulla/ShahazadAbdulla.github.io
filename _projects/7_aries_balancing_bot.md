**Technology Stack:** RISC-V, Aries V3, Control Theory, C++

Designed and built a **self-balancing robot** powered by the **Aries V3 board** from CDAC India and Vega Processors.

- **RISC-V Architecture:** Leveraged the capabilities of the indigenous Aries V3 RISC-V processor for real-time control.
- **System Integration:** Integrated motors, IMUs, and power systems into a compact mobile platform.
- **Control Logic:** Implemented closed-loop control algorithms to maintain upright stability.
