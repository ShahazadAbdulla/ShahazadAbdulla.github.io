**Technology Stack:** C++, RISC-V, I2C, Embedded Systems

Engineered and open-sourced a custom **MPU6050 sensor library** specifically optimized for the **Vega Aries** (RISC-V) development board.

- **Reliable Data Acquisition:** Implemented robust I2C communication to read accelerometer and gyroscope data.
- **Open Source:** Contributed to the open-source community, facilitating easier adoption of RISC-V hardware for robotics applications.
- **Hardware Integration:** Validated on Vega Processors' hardware, acknowledged for contributing to their ecosystem.
