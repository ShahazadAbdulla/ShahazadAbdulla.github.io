**Role:** Captain and Head of Autonomous Systems
**Competition:** BAJA SAEINDIA 2025 & 2026
**Tech Stack:** ROS 2, NVIDIA Jetson, CAN Bus, Arduino, Control Systems

- **Secured All India Rank 7** at aBAJA 2025 (passing all technical and autonomous checks on the first attempt) and **All India Rank 11** at aBAJA 2026 as Captain of a **40-member team**.
- **Architected** the vehicle's distributed, real-time control architecture; integrated a high-level NVIDIA Jetson with multiple low-level Arduino R4 Minima nodes over a robust **CAN bus network**.
- **Engineered** safety-critical firmware, including PID controllers for actuators, ensuring precise vehicle control and adherence to competition rules.
- **Integrated** lane keeping, adaptive cruise control, emergency braking, path following, and traffic light and sign detection, with a focus on system safety and validation.
