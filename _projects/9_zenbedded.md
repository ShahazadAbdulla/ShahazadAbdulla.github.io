**Role:** Core Developer and Organization Member
**Organization:** Open Robotics / ros-controls
**Tech Stack:** C++, ros2_control, Eclipse Zenoh, Zephyr RTOS, CDR serialization

- **Contributing** to an agent-less embedded integration stack that connects **Zephyr**-based microcontrollers with **ros2_control** over **Eclipse Zenoh**, covering transport abstractions plus joint-state and command communication.
- **Developed** zero-allocation, static-offset **CDR serialization** and contributed to embedded discovery, CI, and latency benchmarking.
- **Investigated** a Zenoh-Pico unicast keep-alive teardown race affecting embedded connections.
- Repository: [ros-controls/zenbedded](https://github.com/ros-controls/zenbedded)
