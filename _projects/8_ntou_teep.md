**Role:** TEEP Research Intern, Mechanical and Mechatronic Engineering
**Organization:** National Taiwan Ocean University (NTOU), Keelung, Taiwan
**Tech Stack:** ROS 2 Jazzy, NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano, STM32F446RE, KiCad, Eclipse Zenoh, Gazebo Harmonic

- **Developed** a robotics system integrating NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano, ROS 2 Jazzy, embedded control hardware, containerized software, and a **Gazebo Harmonic digital twin** of an Ackermann-steered robot.
- **Designed** a custom KiCad PCB for the STM32F446RE controller and integrated steering control with ROS 2 through **Zenbedded** and **Eclipse Zenoh**.
- **Built** Android-to-robot streaming of camera, IMU, and GPS data over Zenoh for teleoperation and logging.
