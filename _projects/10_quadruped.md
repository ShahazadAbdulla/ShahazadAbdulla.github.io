**Technology Stack:** ROS 2 Humble, Nav2, SLAM Toolbox, RF2O, EKF, Gazebo

Migrated the **Hiwonder PuppyPi** quadruped onto a full ROS 2 navigation stack.

- **Navigation & SLAM:** Integrated **Nav2**, **SLAM Toolbox**, **RF2O**, and EKF-based sensor fusion for localisation and path planning.
- **Teleoperation:** Implemented reliable remote teleoperation and worked through servo stiction and driver-level issues.
- **Agents:** Explored NASA JPL's **ROSA** agent for robotic task execution.
