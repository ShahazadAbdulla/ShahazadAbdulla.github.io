**Technology Stack:** Computer Vision, AI, Python

Designed a **Smart Interactive Mirror** that enhances the user experience by leveraging **Artificial Intelligence** and **Computer Vision**.

The mirror analyzes the user's facial expressions and body language upon entering the room to provide personalized feedback and interactive experiences. It aims to transform a standard reflective surface into a smart assistant for homes, offices, or gyms.
