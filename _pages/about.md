Hello! I'm a Robotics and Automation Engineering undergraduate focused on robotics systems engineering — autonomous systems, embedded and edge AI, real-time communication, control, and perception.

My work spans the full stack, from firmware and custom PCBs to SLAM and deployment on embedded hardware like the **NVIDIA Jetson**: distributed control over **CAN bus**, microcontrollers over **Eclipse Zenoh** and **micro-ROS**, and high-fidelity simulation in **Gazebo** and **Isaac Sim**.

I'm currently a **TEEP research intern at National Taiwan Ocean University (Taiwan)**, where I'm building an Ackermann-steered robot stack around ROS 2 Jazzy, a custom STM32 board, and a Gazebo digital twin. Earlier, during my internship at **CSIR-NAL**, I designed a sensor fusion system that reduced UGV trajectory error by over **90%** and co-authored the resulting paper at **ARMS 2025**. At **Intel**, I engineered an AI-driven quality control pipeline achieving **100% precision**. I lead **Team Equinox** (BAJA SAEINDIA) as Captain, taking the team to **All India Rank 7 in 2025** and **Rank 11 in 2026**.

In open source, I'm a core developer on **Zenbedded** (ros2_control + Zephyr + Zenoh), have merged contributions into **control_msgs** and **ros_gz_bridge**, and ranked **3rd globally in 2025** and **4th in 2026** in the ROS 2 release "Tutorial Party" with over **230 issues** closed.

Feel free to explore my projects or get in touch!
